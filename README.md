## Featured Innovations

### 1. IoT-Enabled Robot for Gas Pipe Detection

**Reference:** `202421078054`

This invention explores the use of connected robotic systems to support inspection and detection tasks involving gas pipelines. The engineering motivation is to improve access to difficult-to-inspect locations and support more systematic monitoring of pipeline infrastructure.

**Key engineering themes**
- Integration of robotic mobility and sensing
- IoT-enabled monitoring and communication
- Pipeline inspection in constrained environments
- Potential applications in infrastructure safety and maintenance

[Explore patent documentation](./202421078054%20-%20IoT%20enabled%20robot%20for%20detecting%20gas%20pipe/)

### 2. Pipeline Traversing Robot

**Reference:** `202521045231`

This invention investigates a robotic platform intended to move through pipeline environments, where limited clearance, surface contact, and changes in pipe geometry create mobility challenges.

**Key engineering themes**
- Mechanical locomotion within pipes
- Traction and contact mechanisms
- Design constraints for confined spaces
- Robotic inspection and maintenance applications

The repository also contains documentation relating to patent examination and technical responses.

[Explore patent documentation](./202521045231%20-%20A%20pipeline%20traversing%20robot/)

### 3. Adjustable Fidget Spinner Base for Pipe Climbing

**Reference:** `458534-001`

This design concerns an adjustable mechanical base associated with pipe-climbing applications. Its inclusion reflects the importance of component-level mechanical design in developing robotic systems that interact with cylindrical surfaces.

**Key engineering themes**
- Adjustable mechanical geometry
- Pipe-contact and climbing mechanisms
- Compact mechanical design
- Component-level robotics innovation

[Explore design documentation](./458534-001%20-%20Adjustable%20fidget%20spinner%20base%20for%20pipe%20climbing/)
